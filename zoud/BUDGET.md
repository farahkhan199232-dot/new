## D. Sustainability
- Year-one revenue targets come from **B2B licences** (banks, fintechs, employers' wellbeing
  programmes) and **classroom licences**; the consumer web/mobile version stays free.
- Ongoing cost after launch is small (hosting about $50/month, content updates by the lead developer).
- If funded at a lower level, lines 4, 6 and 7 are deferred first; the learning design, Arabic
  localisation, adviser review, accessibility audit and pilot are protected.
